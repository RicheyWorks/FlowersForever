# Getting started with FlowersForever

One page to get your farm going. You can open it again anytime from **Help → Getting started guide…**

## 1. Install

1. Double-click the FlowersForever installer (.msi) and follow the steps.
2. This test version is not code-signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**, only for an installer you got directly from Richey. If antivirus or anything else warns you, stop and ask him.
3. Open FlowersForever from the Start menu. Your records stay on this computer, so you need no account and no internet.

## 2. Choose your farm

At the first start, pick your farm type: **Wildflower farm** for cut flowers, **Rose & events** for roses and wedding work, or one of the other types. To change or combine types later, use **File → Choose or combine farm types…**

## 3. Your first ten minutes

On a flower farm, open **Help → Start here…** and pick one start (other farm types begin on the first tab of their workspace):

- Log today’s first cut: the crop and the quantity are enough.
- Bring in the spreadsheet you already keep: **File → Bring in a spreadsheet…** (CSV or Excel).
- Add one product by hand.

You do not need every screen on day one.

## 4. A normal day

- Morning: **Help → Today’s reminders…** shows what is due.
- After cutting: record it in the Harvest Log.
- Selling to florists: **File → Share availability with florists…** copies your stock list, saves it as a CSV file, or starts an email.

## 5. Keep your records safe

- Once: **File → Automatic backups…** and choose a folder or a USB drive.
- Before big changes: **File → Back up now…**
- If something goes wrong: **File → Restore from a backup…** checks the backup and saves your current records first.

## 6. Updates and help

- FlowersForever checks for updates when it opens. Before installing one, it saves a checked backup, and if the new version does not start correctly it goes back to the old one with your records. **Help → Check for updates…** checks now or turns the automatic checks off.
- Stuck or something looks wrong? **Help → Contact / Report a problem…** starts a report that already includes your app version and Windows details.
